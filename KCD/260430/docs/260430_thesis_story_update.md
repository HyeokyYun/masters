# Thesis Story Update After 260430 Meeting

## Recommended Main Line

Keep the thesis centered on store-level lifecycle prediction:

> Early post-entry transaction patterns can predict whether a store later moves
> toward Growth, Stable, or Decline, but the validation must account for
> calendar seasonality.

This is the cleanest MSc thesis line because it directly addresses the advisor's
methodological concern and avoids over-expanding the dissertation into a city
economy paper.

## What Changes

The prior fixed late-window outcome is not wrong as an exploratory baseline, but
it is vulnerable to seasonality. The updated analysis should report whether the
main conclusion survives when early and target windows are matched by month.

If the seasonal-window results remain consistent, the thesis gains a stronger
robustness argument:

> The predictive signal is not only an artifact of January-to-summer or
> summer-to-winter seasonality.

## Role of LEVI and EWS

LEVI and EWS remain valuable, but their academic role should be limited in the
current thesis.

- LEVI: useful bridge to regional economic vitality, best kept as validation or
  future journal extension.
- Golden Cross: useful supporting mechanism for why new-customer inflow matters.
- EWS: useful application framing, but not the main academic contribution unless
  expanded into a decision-support artifact paper.
- Hybrid representation: strongest add-on for a paper because it directly shows
  predictive improvement from trajectory states and change-point features.

## Recommended Defense Framing

For the defense, use the seasonal-window analysis as a methodological correction:

1. The first analysis showed meaningful early predictive signal.
2. The meeting identified a seasonality concern in the outcome window.
3. The revised analysis compares the same calendar windows across years.
4. The thesis conclusion should rely on the revised seasonal-window result.
